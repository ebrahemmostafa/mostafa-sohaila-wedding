# Google Flow (Veo) — Sea Garden hero video

1. Open https://labs.google/flow and sign in with your own Google account.
2. New project → **Frames to Video** → upload `sea-garden-9x16.png` as the first frame, aspect ratio **9:16 (portrait)**.
3. Paste this prompt:

> Cinematic slow dolly-in down a glowing wedding aisle at night, toward a golden arch at the end. The scene starts almost dark; warm lights switch on one by one from the candles in the foreground, along the aisle, up to the arch. Then tall searchlight beams shoot up from behind the arch into the night sky and slowly sweep and cross. Soft pink roses, white chairs, gentle golden floating sparkles, light haze, luxurious, elegant, smooth camera, no people, no text, photorealistic, 4K.

4. Download the best take as MP4, rename it to `sea-garden-hero.mp4` and replace `assets/videos/sea-garden-hero.mp4`
   (or send it to Claude and it will swap it in and make the poster/share images from it).
